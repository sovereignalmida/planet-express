WHY THIS WORKS AT 88

  • The stack is the unit, not the container. 15 cards instead of 88 rows. Cards size to their contents, so dockge with one container takes one line and media with sixteen takes four — no fixed-height grid forcing them to match.
  • Green says nothing, so it costs nothing. A healthy container is an LED and its name. running(healthy) vanishes — a status word appears only when the status isn't green, which makes the exceptions findable instead of camouflaged.
  • Trouble sorts to the front. Stacks order by worst member, then by size. You never scroll to find the broken one, and the rail above the grid names it outright.
  • Two densities, one layout. NAMED when you're working, DOTS when you're checking — 88 containers in about 620px. Phones default to DOTS; a tap expands one card in place.
  • Attention filter over search. With 15 stacks you don't need to search, you need "show me the 2 that matter". The filter collapses to those, and reads as a reassurance when the answer is none.
  • Everything stays clickable. Chip → container detail; card header → stack actions. The list's one virtue was that every container was reachable, and that's preserved.
